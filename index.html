Reading the BPM chart

BPM refers to quarter notes in the original recording. Choose the tap value in Settings.

Averages: beats divided by duration, including fermatas. Trend and section detection ignore the effect of fermatas. Sections represent sustained tempo changes, not musical form.

Detection and expression are editable estimates. In expressive piano, notes and beats can be ambiguous: use a tempo hint or a few manual taps. Check uncertain beats under Review.
